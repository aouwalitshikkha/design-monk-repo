Summary of Recommendations

1Add <lastmod> timestamps to all sitemap entries in ISO 8601 format and configure automatic updates
2Remove sitewide homepage canonical from paginated pages; set section home page canonical (e.g., /projects, /blog) and add rel="prev"/"next"
3Add <meta name="robots" content="index,follow"> on article pages and noindex,follow on category pages
4Implement article:published_time, article:modified_time, and article:author Open Graph meta tags on all articles
5Fix breadcrumb: replace static "Blog details" with dynamic article title in Blog Home > blog > [title] format
6Apply noindex,follow to blank category pages and remove them from the XML sitemap
7Delete all empty categories entirely to prevent SEO dilution and thin content signals
8Disable video autoplay or set to muted to improve user experience and Core Web Vitals
9Implement email format validation on the submission form and document the lead capture & nurture process
10Upload branded YouTube videos ("What is Design Monks", "How We Use AI", portfolio walkthroughs) to fix AI Overview unrelated video issue
11Add YouTube link to all pages that display social media icons (homepage, About Us, footer, etc.)
12Link the category name at the end of each blog post to its category archive page for better crawlability
