Summary of Recommendations

1Add <lastmod> timestamps to all sitemap entries in ISO 8601 format
2Implement self-referencing canonical on each pagination page; remove random query params
3Add rel="next" and rel="prev" to pagination sequences
4Add <meta name="robots" content="index,follow"> on article pages; noindex,follow on category pages
5Implement article:published_time, article:modified_time, article:author meta tags
6Fix breadcrumb: replace "Blog details" with actual article title
7Set blank category pages to noindex,follow and remove from sitemap
8Configure automatic lastmod/OG tag updates via CMS or build process
