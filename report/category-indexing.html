Critical Issue

Category Page Indexing

Category pages contain no content but remain indexed, wasting crawl budget and potentially triggering thin-content quality issues.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: Several category pages (e.g., /projects-categories/legal-services and similar) contain no meaningful content but are still being indexed by search engines. These blank category pages provide no value to users and dilute the site's content quality signal.

Affected URLs

  • https://www.designmonk.co/projects-categories/legal-services
  • Additional blank category pages (similar pattern)

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Blank Category Pages Indexed Critical Empty category pages provide zero user value. Indexing them wastes crawl budget and may trigger Google's thin-content algorithms.
Included in Sitemap Critical Including blank category pages in the sitemap sends mixed signals — search engines are told these pages are important when they have no substantive content.
Duplicate Content Risk Warning Multiple empty category pages with similar structure may be seen as duplicate or low-effort content by search engines.

Recommendations

1

Apply noindex,follow to All Empty Category Pages

Add <meta name="robots" content="noindex,follow"> to all category pages that lack meaningful content. This prevents indexation while still allowing crawlers to follow links.

2

Remove from XML Sitemap

Remove all blank category pages from the XML sitemap. Only include pages with valuable, indexable content that you want search engines to discover and rank.

3

Populate or Consolidate Categories

Alternatively, populate empty category pages with meaningful content such as category descriptions, featured articles, or curated project lists. If a category cannot be populated, consider consolidating or removing it entirely.
