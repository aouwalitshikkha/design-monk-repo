Missing

Article Open Graph Meta Data

Single article pages lack required Open Graph article meta tags, reducing social sharing richness and discoverability.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: Article pages are missing essential Open Graph metadata that platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter rely on for rich previews. The following tags are not present:

Missing Elements:

<meta property="article:published_time" content="2026-05-05T10:55:15Z">
<meta property="article:modified_time" content="2026-05-05T10:55:15Z">
<meta property="article:author" content="Author Name">

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Missing published_time Critical Without published_time, platforms cannot determine content freshness, reducing social sharing quality.
Missing modified_time Warning Updated content may not be reflected as fresh in social previews without modified_time.
Missing article:author Warning Author attribution in social previews helps establish credibility and E-E-A-T signals.

Recommendations

1

Implement All Article Meta Tags

Add the complete set of Open Graph article meta tags with accurate timestamps and author information to every article page template.

<meta property="article:published_time" content="2026-05-05T10:55:15Z">
<meta property="article:modified_time" content="2026-05-05T10:55:15Z">
<meta property="article:author" content="Author Name">
2

Configure Automatic Timestamp Updates

Set up the CMS to automatically update article:modified_time whenever content is edited. This ensures social platforms always see the latest freshness signal.

3

Assign Author Credentials

Each article should have a named author with credentials. This supports E-E-A-T signals and improves trustworthiness in search results and social platforms.
