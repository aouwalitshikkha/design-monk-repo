Warning

Missing YouTube Links

YouTube links should be added on pages where social media links are displayed.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: The website displays social media icons/links on various pages but does not include a YouTube channel link. Adding YouTube to these social link sections helps grow the channel, improves brand visibility, and provides Google with authoritative video references.

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Missing from Social Link Sections Warning Pages that display social media icons/links do not include YouTube, missing an opportunity to grow the channel and improve brand visibility.

Recommendations

1

Add YouTube to All Social Link Sections

Ensure every page that has social media icons includes a YouTube link. This includes homepage, About Us, Contact, Blog, and footer sections.
