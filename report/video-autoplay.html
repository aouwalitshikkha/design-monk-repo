Critical Issue

Video Autoplay & User Experience

Autoplay functionality negatively impacts SEO performance and degrades user experience metrics.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: The homepage implements video autoplay on page load. Autoplaying video with sound degrades user experience — users may leave immediately, increasing bounce rate. It also consumes bandwidth, slows page load, and negatively impacts Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP).

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Video Autoplay with Sound Critical Autoplay with sound annoys users, increases bounce rate, and consumes bandwidth. It also delays LCP and harms Core Web Vitals scores.

Recommendations

1

Disable Video Autoplay Entirely

Remove the autoplay attribute from video embeds. Let users choose to play videos when they want. This reduces initial page load size and improves LCP.

2

Use Muted Autoplay as Fallback

If autoplay is essential for design purposes, ensure all videos use the muted attribute. Modern browsers block autoplay with sound, but muted autoplay is allowed and less intrusive.

<video autoplay muted loop playsinline>
  <source src="hero-video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>
