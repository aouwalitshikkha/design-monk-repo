Critical Issue

AI Overview Video Reference

Google AI Overview is showing a video reference unrelated to Design Monks. Branded video content needs to be uploaded to reclaim this space.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: In Google's AI Overview search results, a video reference appears that is not related to Design Monks. This misrepresents the brand, confuses potential clients, and sends mixed signals about the company's expertise and authority.

Why This Matters

Google's AI Overview generates answers by pulling from multiple sources. If no relevant video content from Design Monks exists, Google surfaces competitor or unrelated videos instead. Publishing your own branded video content gives Google a clear, authoritative source to reference.

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Unrelated Video in AI Overview Critical Unrelated video content confuses users and damages brand perception. It also means Design Monks is not controlling its brand narrative in AI search results.
Missing Branded Video Content Critical Without any branded video content on YouTube, Google has no authoritative Design Monks video to reference in AI Overviews or rich results.

Recommendations

1

Create a YouTube Channel for Design Monks

Set up a branded YouTube channel. This gives Google a trusted source to pull video content from for AI Overviews and rich search features.

2

Upload Foundational Brand Videos

Start with basic videos that establish the brand:

-"What is Design Monks?" — Brand introduction
-"How Design Monks Changes the Design Ecosystem" — Brand vision
-"How Design Monks Uses AI" — AI capabilities showcase
-Portfolio walkthroughs and case studies
3

Add YouTube Links to the Website

Embed or link the YouTube channel on the homepage, About Us page, and relevant portfolio pages. Use VideoObject schema markup to help Google understand and surface the content.
