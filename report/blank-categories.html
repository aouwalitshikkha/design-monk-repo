Critical Issue

Blank Categories Diluting SEO

Many empty categories exist on the site with no content. Blank categories dilute SEO efforts and should be removed.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: The site has numerous category pages that contain zero content. These empty shells offer no value to users, waste crawl budget, and send poor quality signals to search engines. Every blank category on the site weakens overall SEO performance.

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Multiple Empty Categories Critical Empty categories provide no value and signal low site quality to search engines. They dilute the site's topical authority.
Wasted Crawl Budget Critical Googlebot wastes time crawling empty category pages instead of discovering valuable content. This reduces crawl efficiency across the site.
Thin Content Signals Warning Multiple blank pages can trigger Google's thin content algorithms, potentially impacting rankings for the entire site.

Recommendations

1

Remove Empty Categories Entirely

Delete all category pages that have no associated content. Empty categories should not exist — they add no value and actively harm SEO by diluting site quality signals.

2

Audit All Categories

Review every category in the CMS. For each category, either populate it with quality content or delete it. Do not keep empty categories as placeholders.

3

Implement Category Creation Policy

Establish a policy that new categories are only created when there is ready content to populate them. This prevents accumulation of empty categories going forward.
