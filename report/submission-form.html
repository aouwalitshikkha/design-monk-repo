Warning

Submission Form Verification

Form functionality requires verification. Check if it works and how leads are handled.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: The form has only an email field. Email validation must be confirmed — invalid email formats should be rejected with clear feedback. Additionally, how leads are captured and nurtured after submission needs to be documented.

Verification Checklist

? Does the form submit successfully without errors?
? Is email validation working? Invalid email formats should be rejected.
? Where do form submissions go? Email, CRM, database?
? Is there a confirmation or thank-you page after submission?
? How are leads nurtured after capture? Email sequence, phone call, or manual follow-up?

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Unverified Form Functionality Warning If the form is broken, potential leads cannot reach the business, resulting in lost opportunities and wasted traffic.
Unknown Lead Handling Process Warning Even if the form works, if leads are not tracked or followed up properly, the business cannot measure ROI or convert inquiries.

Recommendations

1

Implement Email Validation

Add proper email format validation. Invalid emails (e.g., missing @, invalid domain) should be rejected with a clear error message before submission. Use both client-side and server-side validation.

2

Document Lead Capture & Nurture Process

Establish how captured leads are nurtured: welcome email sequence, CRM assignment, response time SLA, and follow-up cadence. Track conversion from form submission to qualified lead.

3

Add Confirmation & Tracking

Show a confirmation message or thank-you page after successful submission. Integrate with Google Analytics to track form submissions as conversions and measure lead generation performance.
