Submission Form Verification

Form functionality requires verification. Check if it works and how leads are handled.

Site-Specific Observations

Observation: The submission form functionality has not been verified. It is unknown whether form submissions are reaching the intended destination, whether validation works correctly, and how leads are managed after submission.

Verification Checklist

? Does the form submit successfully without errors?
? Are validation messages shown for empty or invalid fields?
? Where do form submissions go? Email, CRM, database?
? Is there a confirmation or thank-you page after submission?
? How are leads tracked and followed up?

Impact Assessment

Issue Priority Rationale
Unverified Form Functionality Warning If the form is broken, potential leads cannot reach the business, resulting in lost opportunities and wasted traffic.
Unknown Lead Handling Process Warning Even if the form works, if leads are not tracked or followed up properly, the business cannot measure ROI or convert inquiries.

Recommendations

1

Test Form Submission End-to-End

Submit a test entry through every form on the site. Verify that data reaches the intended destination (email, CRM, database) and that confirmation messages display correctly.

2

Document Lead Handling Process

Establish and document how leads are managed: who receives notifications, response time targets, follow-up procedures, and how leads are tracked in the CRM or spreadsheet.

3

Implement Error Handling & Confirmation

Add proper error handling for failed submissions and clear success/confirmation messages. Consider integrating with Google Analytics to track form submissions as conversions.
